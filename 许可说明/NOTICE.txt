removed parenthetical gloss qualifiers; grouped Chinese equivalents;
added corpus occurrence forms, source links and manually prepared meanings
and collocations. This derivative lexicon is distributed under CC BY-SA 3.0.
Dictionary data is embedded in content.json / the offline HTML. Application
source code and the user's personal annotations are separate from this data.
